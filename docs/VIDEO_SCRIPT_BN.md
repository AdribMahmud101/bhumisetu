## Editorial controls

- Record actual app behavior; do not mock extraction responses or insert fabricated verification results.
- Confirm the current parcel evidence before retaining the 20-versus-24 example. If unavailable, adapt the script to the actual recorded synthetic example rather than invent a screen.
- Use long, readable app views with short camera reframes. Allow stable reading time after each transition.
- Caption chunks may be shorter than a scene; keep their intervals disjoint and inside that scene's speech window.
- Priya voice was generated per scene. `videos/bhumisetu-demo/VOICE_TIMING.json` records the measured take lengths and confirms that all eight lines fit within their speech windows without overlap.
- The captured application clips are muted; the final video carries only the Priya narration. No music was added.
- Keep sample data identified as fictional and account credentials, session cookies, API keys, and private developer panels out of the picture.

Build and recording evidence, source clip ranges and render checks are recorded in `videos/bhumisetu-demo/`. Final delivery: `videos/bhumisetu-demo/renders/BhumiSetu_90s_Bangla_Priya.mp4`.
