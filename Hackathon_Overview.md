### Problem Tracks (Select One)

1. **P1: Digitize Old Records**: Convert scanned Bengali Khatian, Porcha, and Deed records (printed or handwritten) into searchable digital records[cite: 5].
2. **P2: Verify Deeds & Ownership**: Implement cryptographic validation, QR codes, hashing, or digital signatures to verify documents and prevent tampering[cite: 6].
3. **P3: Catch Fraud & Double-Selling**: Detect duplicate sales, fraudulent transfers, and discrepancies between deed registration and mutation records[cite: 6].
4. **P4: Smart e-Mutation & Registry**: Seamlessly link deed registration with mutation workflows using automated cross-checks and status tracking[cite: 6].
5. **P5: Check Before You Buy**: Citizen-facing verification tool to check plot ownership history, pending court litigation, and khas/acquisition status prior to purchase[cite: 6].
6. **P6: Your Own Idea**: Any original solution falling under the general track theme, subject to organizer approval[cite: 6].

### Submission & Presentation Requirements

**Round 1 (Online Submission on the Platform)**

* **Concept Note**: PDF, maximum 3 pages (problem description, national significance, solution architecture, tech stack, scalability, and team details)[cite: 7].
* **GitHub Repository**: Public link containing working prototype code, commit history, and a README detailing setup instructions, datasets used, and AI tools utilized[cite: 7, 8].
* **Demo Video**: Maximum 90 seconds prototype demonstration[cite: 7].
* **Live Deployment Link**: Optional but recommended[cite: 7].
* *Selection*: Up to 25 teams qualify for the Final Round[cite: 7].

**Final Round (In-Person Presentation)**

* **Deliverables**: Updated code on GitHub and a Pitch Deck (PDF, maximum 10 slides)[cite: 7].
* **Format**: 5 minutes live pitch and prototype demo + 3 minutes Q&A with judges[cite: 7].
* **Language**: English or Bangla[cite: 7].
* **Presenters**: Only the two student team members are permitted to speak[cite: 7].

### Critical Rules & Compliance

* **Data Privacy**: Strictly use dummy/synthetic data or organizer-provided sample sets[cite: 8]. Never use real citizen names, NIDs, phone numbers, or real deed records[cite: 8].
* **System Security**: Do not scrape or stress-test live government portals[cite: 8]. Security mechanisms must be tested strictly on self-hosted environments[cite: 8].
* **AI Tool Policy**: Tools like ChatGPT are permitted, provided they are disclosed in the README and students are capable of explaining all submitted code[cite: 8, 9].
* **Disqualification Triggers**: Fabricated data/identity, plagiarism, tampering with commit histories, hitting live systems, or unauthorized team changes[cite: 8].
